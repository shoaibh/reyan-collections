Where everything runs

One app does everything: the shop, the admin and the backend. A few free services handle the jobs we shouldn't build ourselves.

People
CustomersArrive from Instagram via /links on their phones
↓ every visit passes through
CloudflareDNS for reyancollection.in, speed cache, bot check at checkout (Turnstile), hello@ email forwarding, visitor statsFree
YouAdmin at reyancollection.in/admin, on your phone or laptop
Your app on Railway (Singapore)
Next.js + Payload: one app, one deploy Shop: pages are pre-built and refresh when you publish, so they load fast
Admin: Payload's dashboard plus our custom "Today" screen
Backend: checkout, prices recalculated on the server, stock reserved, payment webhooks, GST invoices
Scheduled jobs: every 15 minutes, cancel expired orders and return their stock
Postgres databaseProducts, orders, settings. Same Railway project, on a private network. Nightly backup copied to R2.
≈ $5–8 / month for both (₹450–700)
Services we plug in
Cloudflare R2Product photos (resized copies made on upload) and backupsFree up to 10 GB
RazorpayUPI, cards, netbanking. Card details never touch our server.~2% per prepaid order
ShiprocketPincode check, courier booking, tracking updatesPay per parcel
ResendOrder and shipping emailsFree up to 3,000/month
